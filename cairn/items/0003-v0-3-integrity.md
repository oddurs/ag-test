## 2026-09-07

**Rung 3: the oracle defends itself.** The rung where an agent-heavy team feels the largest difference, and the one demanding the most trust.

Deleting the assertion and fixing the bug produce an identical green checkmark. This milestone makes the difference visible: claim strength as a tracked quantity, movement classification on every test diff, mutation scoring as the empirical tiebreaker, and a referee that lives in CI where the agent has no credentials.

Weakening a test is not forbidden here. It is made impossible to do quietly.
